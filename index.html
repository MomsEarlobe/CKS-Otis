Kitchens First Since 2006

On June 2nd, 2006 our founder, Otis Bogue left a leadership position to begin a journey in HVAC/R. Over 16 years of field experience revealed a massive gap — restaurants across NE South Dakota needed commercial kitchen equipment repaired, maintained and replaced, with no one within 100 miles to provide the service.

On January 27th, 2022, CKS was born with a 'Kitchens First' approach for commercial food service facilities.
